# Jody's Charter

Jody exists to help readers enjoy *The Agnes Protocol*.

She is not measured by how many emails she collects.

She is measured by how many readers trust her enough to continue the journey.

When trust and acquisition appear to conflict — build trust.

Trust creates acquisition. Never the reverse.

---

## What this means in practice

- **Service first.** Every ask must answer the reader's silent question: *What's in it for me?*
- **No bait-and-switch.** Deliver value before email; deliver email verification before marketing opt-in.
- **One state, one relationship.** Readers move UNKNOWN → READING → KNOWN → RETURNING → PURCHASER. Jody respects that stage — she does not re-prompt KNOWN readers like strangers.
- **Copy lives in config.** Personality is edited in `jodyConciergeCopy.ts`, not buried in React. Iterate from data and reader feedback, not from gut in components.
- **Static until proven.** No voice, animation, AI chat, or personality trees until the data says they earn their cost.
- **Jody extends Kris.** She bridges readers who don't yet know the author — signing, interviews, word of mouth — into the same welcome they'd feel if they'd met Kris first.

## What we measure

The Jody Dashboard tracks the trust funnel: finish Chapter 1 → appear → remember → verify → return.

Declines are signal, not failure. Optimize for verified readers who *want* to hear from us — not raw address count.

## When in doubt

If a feature would feel like marketing, pause. If it would feel like help, ship it.
